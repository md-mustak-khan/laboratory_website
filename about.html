About the Laboratory

A joint research initiative bridging natural product biochemistry, molecular biology, and in-vivo pharmacology at the University of Chittagong.

Our Identity

Bridging Natural Products & Applied Bioscience

The Laboratory of Food Science and Nutraceuticals (LFSN) operates within the Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology at the University of Chittagong, Bangladesh. It was founded on the principle that transformative discovery happens when empirical natural product biochemistry is integrated directly with modern computational modeling, functional genomics, and in-vivo validation.

Led jointly by Prof. Dr. Dwaipayan Sikdar and Assoc. Prof. Dr. Suman Mojumder, our group conducts research addressing pressing regional and global challenges in nutritional biochemistry, food security, disease prevention, and applied biotechnology.

Our Mission

To rigorously isolate and characterize bioactive molecules from indigenous botanical and marine sources in Bangladesh, elucidating their molecular mechanisms of action through rigorous wet-bench, in-silico, and in-vivo model systems.

Our Vision

To establish a world-class center of academic excellence in food science, antioxidant pharmacology, and nematode model biology, empowering young Bangladeshi scientists with cutting-edge, reproducible research training.

Guiding Principles

Our Core Values

The scientific culture and standard of excellence that guide every experiment in our laboratory.

Scientific Rigor

Commitment to reproducible methodologies, thorough assay replicates, and transparent data standards.

Mentorship

Empowering young Bangladeshi undergraduate and graduate scholars with hands-on bench and bioinformatics training.

Open Science

Sharing laboratory protocols, publication preprints, and research datasets to foster regional and international collaboration.

Translational Impact

Translating fundamental phytochemical discoveries into evidence-based functional foods and health solutions.

Milestones

Laboratory Development Timeline

Key phases in the evolution of our research group at the University of Chittagong.

2016–2018 · Foundation Phase

Foundational Phytochemistry & Food Analysis

Initiated systematic profiling of native Bangladeshi fruits, wild date palms, and agricultural products for antioxidant and enzymatic capabilities under Prof. Dr. Dwaipayan Sikdar.

2019–2021 · Microbiology & Enzymology

Insect Gut Microbiology & Metagenomics

Expanded into cellulolytic/xylanolytic microbial biodiversity in insect gut ecosystems and enzymology led by Assoc. Prof. Dr. Suman Mojumder.

2022–2024 · Model Organism Integration

C. elegans In-Vivo Testing Suite Established

Equipped specialized temperature-controlled cooled incubators, stereo and fluorescent microscopy suites to study oxidative stress and lifespan effects of plant bioactives using C. elegans.

2025–2026+ · Unified LFSN Era

Comprehensive Integrated Research Ecosystem

Unified joint laboratory infrastructure combining high-throughput computational drug discovery, marine algal bioactives (Ulva lactuca), and international peer-reviewed publication outputs.
