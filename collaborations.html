Capabilities & Research Assay Services

We provide specialized bioassay capabilities, in-vivo model organism phenotyping, and analytical chemistry support for research collaborators, postgraduate projects, and grant consortia.

In-Vivo C. elegans Lifespan Assays

Gold-standard whole-organism testing determining the pro-longevity and anti-aging efficacy of candidate natural extracts and dietary compounds:

  • Kaplan-Meier survival curves & mortality rate kinetics
  • Mean, median, and maximum lifespan span determination
  • Pro-longevity screening for candidate nutraceuticals & botanicals
  • Daily survival scoring & age-dependent survival shifts
  • Synchronized nematode cohorts under temperature-controlled culture
In-Vivo Lifespan

In-Vivo C. elegans Healthspan Assays

Quantitative physiological phenotyping measuring functional vitality, neuromuscular endurance, and delay of age-associated functional decline:

  • Locomotor sinusoidal wave motility & body bending frequency
  • Age-associated neuromuscular decay & movement class scoring
  • Pharyngeal pumping rate (dietary intake & muscle fitness)
  • Swimming thrashing assay & physiological vigor tracking
  • Real-time time-lapse motility analysis on NGM agar
In-Vivo Healthspan

In-Vivo Stress Survival Assays

Comprehensive environmental and chemical challenge tests evaluating cytoprotective and resistance-enhancing bioactivities:

  • Thermal stress survival assays (heat shock at 35°C / 37°C)
  • Acute oxidative stress resistance (Juglone, H2O2, Paraquat)
  • Chemical & environmental stress survival curve profiling
  • Protection quantification against lethal pro-oxidant injury
  • Statistical survival percentage enhancement vs. untreated controls
Stress Resistance

In-Vivo Toxicity & Safety Assays

Rapid and ethical whole-animal toxicological screening evaluating compound safety thresholds, organismal tolerance, and developmental safety:

  • Acute & chronic whole-organism toxicity evaluations
  • Lethal concentration (LC50) & dose-response curve determination
  • Developmental toxicity & larval stage transition monitoring
  • Brood size & reproductive fecundity safety screening
  • Maximum tolerated dose & safety window profiling
Toxicity Screening

Bioactive Component Analysis

Comprehensive phytochemical extraction, liquid-liquid fractionation, and quantitative screening from botanical and marine matrices:

  • Total Phenolic Content (TPC, Folin-Ciocalteu method)
  • Total Flavonoid Content (TFC, Aluminum chloride method)
  • Liquid-liquid solvent partitioning (Hexane, DCM, Ethyl Acetate, Aqueous)
  • Low-temperature vacuum freeze drying (Lyophilization)
  • Crude extract standardization, yield determination & sample prep
Phytochemistry

In-Vitro Antioxidant Assays

High-precision multi-mechanism spectrophotometric screening of free radical scavenging capacities and redox potency:

  • DPPH radical scavenging kinetics & IC50 determination
  • Ferric Reducing Antioxidant Power (FRAP) absorbance kinetics
  • ABTS•+ cation radical decolorization spectrophotometric assays
  • Nitric Oxide (NO) & Hydroxyl radical scavenging assays
  • Multi-concentration comparative antioxidant potency profiling

Collaborative Process

How We Collaborate: 4-Step Project Pathway

From initial hypothesis scoping to joint peer-reviewed publications, our collaborative framework ensures rigorous scientific methodology and transparent partnership.

1

Initial Consultation & Scoping

We discuss your research aims, biological questions, sample quantity, selected bioassays, and project timeline with our Principal Investigators.

2

Standardized Assay Execution

Samples are processed with standardized protocol controls, technical replicates, and automated instrumentation under strict quality control.

3

Data Analysis & Phenotyping

We generate publication-ready data sets: survival curves, IC50 values, statistical analyses (ANOVA), photomicrographs, and qPCR plots.

4

Joint Publication & Dissemination

Results are translated into co-authored scientific manuscripts submitted to high-impact peer-reviewed journals, thesis chapters, or grant reports.
