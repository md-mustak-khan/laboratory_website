Visiting Us

Campus Commute & Directions

How to reach our laboratory from Chattogram city center.

1. University Shuttle Train

Departs regularly from Chattogram Railway Station (Battali Station) and Sholoshahar Station directly to Chittagong University Campus Station.

2. Bus & Highway Transit

Direct campus buses and local commuter buses operate frequently from Muradpur, 2 No. Gate, and Oxygen Mor heading towards Fatehpur/CU Gate.

3. Department Location

Once on campus, head to the Faculty of Biological Sciences Building (3rd Floor), Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology.

Department of BMB & CRE Research Suites

Located on the 3rd Floor of the Faculty of Biological Sciences Building, Department of Biochemistry & Molecular Biology (BMB), University of Chittagong — housing our specialized laboratory suites alongside the Central Research Excellence (CRE) facility.

Laboratory of Food Science and Nutraceuticals

The Laboratory of Food Science and Nutraceuticals (LFSN) is an academic research laboratory within the Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, University of Chittagong, Bangladesh, jointly led by Prof. Dr. Dwaipayan Sikdar and Assoc. Prof. Dr. Suman Mojumder.
