Facilities & Equipment

Core instruments and shared research resources available to CNAB researchers.

Core instruments

Major equipment

Biosafety Cabinet

Sterile workspace for bacterial culture preparation used as C. elegans food and for clean sample handling.

Centrifuge

High-speed separation of biological samples by centrifugal force for fractionation and clarification.

Cooled Incubator

Temperature-controlled unit for C. elegans maintenance, assay incubation, and culture growth.

DNA/RNA Workstation

Contamination-controlled environment for nucleic acid extraction and sample preparation.

Freeze Dryer

Lyophilization system for gentle drying and long-term preservation of biological samples.

Homogenizer

Mechanical disruption and uniform sample processing for tissue and extract preparation.

Hotplate Stirrer

Controlled heating and stirring of liquids for reagent preparation and reactions.

Lab Rocker

Gentle agitating platform for even mixing and incubation of tubes and plates.

Stereomicroscope

Low-magnification tool for C. elegans visualization, specimen inspection, and sample handling.

Water Bath

Precise temperature incubation of tubes and reagents for assays and sample warming.

Analytical Balance

High-precision mass measurement for accurate weighing of chemicals and samples.

Shared resources

Shared resources

Computing cluster

Shared high-performance computing resources for bioinformatics, sequence analysis, and reproducible research workflows.

Cell culture facility

Dedicated tissue-culture space with biosafety cabinets, CO₂ incubators, and inverted microscopy for in-vitro studies.

Sample preparation lab

Shared space for extraction, fractionation, and sample preparation for both food chemistry and molecular biology workflows.

Cold storage & biobank

Ultra-low temperature freezers and organised sample storage for long-term preservation of biological and extract samples.

CNAB

The Centre for Nutraceutical and Applied Biosciences (CNAB) is a joint academic research centre within the Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, University of Chittagong, Bangladesh.
