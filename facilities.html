Facilities, Infrastructure & Core Instrumentation

Advanced experimental capabilities powered by our dedicated wet-bench facilities alongside key collaborative research project grants (including the HEAT Project and HEQEP sub-projects accessible to our laboratory) and the Central Research Excellence (CRE) facility of the Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology (BMB), University of Chittagong.

Departmental & Project Ecosystem

Research Infrastructure & Collaborative Environment

Our experimental workflows integrate our dedicated departmental laboratory with the Central Research Excellence (CRE) facility (serving all research labs within the Department of Biochemistry & Molecular Biology) and collaborative research project suites accessible to designated partner laboratories including LFSN:

Dedicated Laboratory

LFSN Wet-Bench Suite

Our departmental laboratory housing natural product extraction, kinetic spectrophotometry, lyophilization, analytical balances, and synchronized C. elegans bioassays.

HEAT & HEQEP Projects

Collaborative Project Facilities

High-throughput Real-Time Quantitative PCR (qPCR), gradient thermal cyclers, and automated nucleic acid systems accessible to participating project laboratories including our lab.

Cold Storage Chain

Cold Storage & Biobanking

Comprehensive cold preservation chain spanning ultra-low −80°C biobanking, −40°C deep freezers, −20°C/−10°C freezers, and +4°C cold storage.

Dept. of BMB Central Facility

Central Research Excellence (CRE)

Centralized departmental facility serving all research laboratories within the Department of Biochemistry & Molecular Biology, housing multi-laser Flow Cytometry, fluorescence microscopy, and ChemiDoc imaging.

Experimental Pipeline

Integrated Research Workflow: From Raw Matrix to Molecular Mechanism

Our laboratory instrumentation operates as an integrated analytical pipeline connecting natural product extraction, biochemical screening, in-vivo model organism validation, and transcriptomic gene expression profiling.

1. Extraction & Lyophilization Tissue homogenization, rotary concentration, and low-temperature freeze drying.
2. In-Vitro Bioassays UV-Vis spectrophotometry for antioxidant kinetics (DPPH/FRAP) and enzyme inhibition.
3. In-Vivo Model Testing C. elegans synchronized culture at 20°C, lifespan assays, and stereomicroscopy.
4. Molecular Validation Total RNA purification, cDNA synthesis, and Real-Time qPCR gene expression quantification.
