Behind the Lens: How We Capture Our Science

Explore how high-resolution digital microscopy, kinetic spectrophotometry, and team collaboration power our discoveries.

1. In-Vivo Bioimaging & Motility

We capture real-time time-lapse movement and fluorescence imaging in C. elegans to quantify neuromuscular vitality, oxidative stress reduction, and healthy longevity curves with statistical precision.

2. Wet-Bench Analytical Partitioning

From marine macroalgae to indigenous fruits, we document solvent fractionation, freeze-drying lyophilization, and optical absorbance assays that isolate potent bioactive metabolites.

3. Academic Culture & Knowledge Exchange

Our photographic records reflect a vibrant research atmosphere: bi-weekly journal clubs, Master's thesis presentations, scientific milestone celebrations, and national symposiums.

Laboratory of Food Science and Nutraceuticals

The Laboratory of Food Science and Nutraceuticals (LFSN) is an academic research laboratory within the Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, University of Chittagong, Bangladesh, jointly led by Prof. Dr. Dwaipayan Sikdar and Assoc. Prof. Dr. Suman Mojumder.
