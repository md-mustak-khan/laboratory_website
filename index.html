Leadership & Faculty

Principal Investigators

Our laboratory brings together two complementary research streams under joint academic leadership, bridging natural product biochemistry and computational life sciences.

Prof. Dr. Dwaipayan Sikdar

Principal Investigator · Professor, Department of Biochemistry & Molecular Biology, University of Chittagong
Phytochemistry Antioxidant Profiling Food Bioactives Nutritional Biochemistry

Specializing in the extraction, chemical profiling, and bioactivity assessment of indigenous fruits, vegetables, and medicinal plants from Bangladesh, with a focus on functional nutrition and human health.

Assoc. Prof. Dr. Suman Mojumder

Principal Investigator · Associate Professor, Department of Biochemistry & Molecular Biology, University of Chittagong
C. elegans In-Vivo Model Molecular Biology Insect Gut Microbiology Computational Biology

Focusing on molecular pharmacology, insect digestive enzymology, and evaluating antioxidant bioactivity and stress response pathways using the Caenorhabditis elegans model organism system.

Scientific Scope

Core Research Pillars

View Detailed Themes →

1. Phytochemistry & Nutraceuticals

Isolation, characterization, and quantification of bioactive molecules from indigenous botanical sources and medicinal plants.

Explore details →

2. Antioxidant Analysis

Quantitative profiling of total phenolics, flavonoids, reducing power, and free radical scavenging assays (DPPH, ABTS, FRAP).

Explore details →

3. Bioinformatics & CADD

In-silico docking, molecular dynamics, network pharmacology, and ADMET profiling of candidate natural compounds.

Explore details →

4. Functional Genomics

Gene expression profiling, stress response pathways, and molecular mechanisms underlying response to bioactive diets.

Explore details →

5. Gut Microbiology & Enzymes

Characterization of cellulolytic and xylanolytic bacterial communities inhabiting insect gut ecosystems.

Explore details →

6. In Vivo C. elegans Studies

Evaluating biological effects on longevity, stress resistance, and oxidative protection using C. elegans.

Explore details →

Laboratory of Food Science and Nutraceuticals

The Laboratory of Food Science and Nutraceuticals (LFSN) is an academic research laboratory within the Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, University of Chittagong, Bangladesh, jointly led by Prof. Dr. Dwaipayan Sikdar and Assoc. Prof. Dr. Suman Mojumder.
