Research Admissions & Student Intake

Student research intake is conducted exclusively for undergraduate students of the Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, University of Chittagong, leading into advanced Master's (M.S.) thesis dissertation projects.

Laboratory Intake & Project and Thesis Policy: Our laboratory takes only undergraduate students from the Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, University of Chittagong. After completing their undergraduate (B.Sc. Honours) degree in the department, trained students continue their advanced Master's (M.S.) Thesis or research project in this laboratory under faculty supervision.
Academic Research Progression

Our Departmental Student Progression Pathway

How students join, train, and conduct thesis dissertations in our laboratory:

Stage 1 · Departmental Intake

Undergraduate Research Induction

Early 4th Year B.Sc. (Honours) · BMB CU

Undergraduate students apply through our departmental intake to receive intensive bench training in sterile protocols, NGM agar preparation, C. elegans maintenance, spectrophotometric bioassays, and molecular docking.

Stage 2 · Thesis or Research Project Continuation

Master’s (M.S.) Thesis & Project

Postgraduate Degree · BMB CU

Upon graduating with their B.Sc. (Honours) from BMB CU, students transition into full-time Master's thesis or Research Project—leading independent projects, operating analytical equipment, and authoring peer-reviewed international publications.

Departmental Undergraduate Research Intake (Early 4th Year)

Designed exclusively for early 4th-year undergraduate students currently enrolled in the Department of Biochemistry & Molecular Biology, University of Chittagong (BMB, CU) seeking rigorous laboratory research training, experimental thesis project placement, and subsequent progression into Master's thesis dissertation research.

Frequently Asked Questions

Applicant FAQ

Common questions about joining the laboratory and completing thesis research.

Laboratory research student intake is strictly and exclusively open to undergraduate students from the Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, University of Chittagong (BMB, CU). External students or non-departmental candidates are not admitted for laboratory intake.
Master's (M.S.) thesis positions are not filled through open external recruitment. Instead, undergraduate students who complete their B.Sc. (Honours) degree in the Department of Biochemistry & Molecular Biology at CU and receive foundational research training in our laboratory continue on to carry out their Master's thesis dissertation under faculty supervision.
Students are encouraged to apply during their early 4th year in BMB, CU. This provides adequate time for sterile bench protocol training, C. elegans colony handling, assay calibration, and literature review before starting their formal 4th-year project and Master's thesis.
Prior advanced wet-lab experience is not required. We look for strong foundational understanding in biochemistry, high academic diligence, genuine passion for experimental science, and commitment to maintaining sterile protocols and laboratory safety.
Undergraduate research students commit approximately 8–12 hours per week, planned flexibly around their regular departmental class schedule and exams. Master's thesis students conduct full-time daily research in the laboratory.
Yes, absolutely! Our laboratory strongly prioritizes mentoring student researchers to publish their thesis discoveries in indexed peer-reviewed international journals (e.g., Springer, SciELO, BioTechnologia). Student researchers with solid, validated data are credited with co-authorship or first authorship.

Laboratory of Food Science and Nutraceuticals

The Laboratory of Food Science and Nutraceuticals (LFSN) is an academic research laboratory within the Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, University of Chittagong, Bangladesh, jointly led by Prof. Dr. Dwaipayan Sikdar and Assoc. Prof. Dr. Suman Mojumder.
