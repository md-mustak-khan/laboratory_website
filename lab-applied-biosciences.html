Applied Biosciences & Molecular Biology Focus

Applying molecular biology, bioinformatics, and computational approaches to questions in gene function and biotechnology.

Principal Investigator

Dr. Suman Mojumder

Principal Investigator Kumamoto Univ. Liaison Professor (2024–2029)

Associate Professor, Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, University of Chittagong

Dr. Suman Mojumder is an Associate Professor in the Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology at the University of Chittagong, Bangladesh, where he has been a faculty member since 2010. He earned his B.Sc. (2006) and M.S. (2008) in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology from the University of Chittagong, followed by a Ph.D. in Molecular Cell Biology from Kumamoto University, Japan, in 2019. Alongside his academic tenure, he has served as a Kumamoto University Liaison Professor (2024–2029) and previously worked in clinical and research settings, including roles at the National Healthcare Network (BIRDEM) and Popular Diagnostic Center.

His primary research focuses on molecular cell biology using C. elegans models, phytonutrient and antioxidant profiling of indigenous natural products (such as seaweeds, dates, and Curcuma species), cellulolytic enzymes for biofuel applications, and pandemic disease readiness. Our laboratory integrates experimental biology with modern analytical and computational approaches to strengthen model organism and applied biotechnology research in Bangladesh.

Research

Core research themes

  • Model Organism StudiesUse of C. elegans to evaluate biological effects of plant-derived extracts and synthetic compounds.
  • Bioactivity ScreeningIdentification of compounds with therapeutic relevance through robust biological assay systems.
  • Mechanistic BiologyInvestigation of pathways and responses linked to health, stress, and disease processes.
  • Applied BiotechnologyTranslation of molecular and cellular findings into broader scientific and biomedical applications.
  • Computational BiologyIntegration of data analysis and bioinformatics into experimental biological workflows.
  • Capacity BuildingTraining young researchers in reproducible, modern, and interdisciplinary bioscience methods.
