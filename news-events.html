Dual Celebration · 12 September 2026

Joint Celebration: Prof. Dr. Suman Mojumder's Promotion & Farewell to MEXT Scholar Ovi Sikder

On 12 September 2026, the LFBMH research family gathered for a momentous dual celebration. The laboratory commemorated Principal Investigator Prof. Dr. Suman Mojumder on his elevation to Full Professor of Biochemistry & Molecular Biology, while simultaneously hosting an affectionate farewell ceremony for researcher and alumnus Ovi Sikder upon achieving the prestigious Japanese Government (MEXT) Scholarship at Kyushu University, Japan.

Laboratory of Food Bioactives and Molecular Health

The Laboratory of Food Bioactives and Molecular Health (LFBMH) is an academic research laboratory within the Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, University of Chittagong, Bangladesh, jointly led by Prof. Dr. Dwaipayan Sikdar and Prof. Dr. Suman Mojumder.
