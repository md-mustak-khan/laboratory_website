Alumni

Our alumni describe their time at CNAB as a transformative journey shaped by rigorous training, collaborative mentoring, and hands-on research. They developed confidence in scientific thinking, data analysis, and communication while working across interdisciplinary projects. The lab culture encouraged curiosity, resilience, and teamwork, helping them build strong foundations for careers in academia, industry, and public health.
