Prof. Dr. Dwaipayan Sikdar

Principal Investigator · Professor, Department of Biochemistry & Molecular Biology, University of Chittagong

Prof. Dr. Dwaipayan Sikdar is Professor at the Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, University of Chittagong. He has authored over 20 peer-reviewed articles investigating native Bangladeshi flora, functional foods, antioxidant kinetics, and nutritional biochemistry.

Abhijit Chowdhury, Ph.D.

Senior Scientific Officer & Lab In-charge
IFST, BCSIR, Bangladesh
Session: 1999–2000 M.S. Exam 2008
Email Scholar RG ORCID
Alumni Memory & Reflection

“My journey with the laboratory at the Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, University of Chittagong, began almost 18 years ago when I joined as a thesis student. I was fortunate to be among the early students working in the lab under the close guidance of my respected supervisor and senior lab mates. At that time, the laboratory was still growing, and it feels special to have been a small part of its early journey.

This laboratory was more than just a place to conduct experiments—it was where I took my first real steps into the world of research. I learned not only laboratory techniques and scientific thinking but also patience, discipline, and the value of learning from mistakes. Even after nearly two decades, the memories of those early days remain very close to my heart.”

MS Thesis Research Topic Investigation of digestive enzyme present in the larvae of Diaphania indica (Lepidoptera: Pyralidae).

Md Harunur Rashid

Postdoctoral Researcher (Scientist)
University of Alberta, Canada
Session: 2000–2001 MS Year: 2009
Email Scholar
Alumni Memory & Reflection

“One of my most unforgettable memories from my MS research in 2008 was travelling from place to place, searching for insects for my enzymatic research. Finding enough samples was extremely difficult, which frustrated me deeply. During that challenging period, Prof. Dr. Dwaipayan Sikdar and my other respected professors continuously encouraged me.

After I finally collected the larvae, I reared them in the laboratory and watched them transform into beautiful butterflies. Seeing that transformation with my own eyes was truly amazing—one of those rare moments when scientific research becomes a beautiful life experience. Actually, research is not only about experiments and results; it is about patience, persistence, and curiosity.”

MS Thesis Research Topic Digestive enzyme characterization and physiological biochemistry in insect models (Lepidoptera).

Laboratory of Food Science and Nutraceuticals

The Laboratory of Food Science and Nutraceuticals (LFSN) is an academic research laboratory within the Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, University of Chittagong, Bangladesh, jointly led by Prof. Dr. Dwaipayan Sikdar and Assoc. Prof. Dr. Suman Mojumder.
